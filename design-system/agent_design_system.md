## 🎨 1. iOS 26 Liquid‑Glass Design Language (Mandatory)

### **Overall Aesthetic**
- Liquid-glass surfaces with soft translucency, blur, internal refraction, and layered depth
- Smooth curved shapes resembling melted glass droplets
- High-gloss reflections, gel-like shine, internal glow
- Vibrant gradients with depth, motion, and surface realism
- Premium, minimal, airy Apple aesthetic

### **Components**
- **Cards / Panels:** frosted glass, semi-transparent, inner shadow, edge highlight, soft floating shadow
- **Buttons:** liquid-gel style, glossy reflections, tactile press-in effect
- **Icons:** rounded, gel-based, refracted-gradient icons
- **Navigation bars:** translucent glass, depth blur, subtle bokeh
- **Surfaces:** consistent translucency + internal glow

### **Motion (Representation Only)**
- Fluid spring-like transitions
- Soft parallax
- Ripple touch effects
*(Design notes only — no animation code)*

## 📐 5. Component Design Rules

### **Cards**
- 24–32 blur radius
- 6–12% border glow
- 1px inner-light stroke
- Shadow: 0px 8px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)

### **Buttons**
- Liquid-gel curvature
- Multi-layer shine highlight
- Depth shadow + inner glow using palette colors
- Use gradients #1 or #2

### **Icons**
- Soft edges, gel shine
- Neon aqua or electric cyan accent
- Dark mode: inner-reflective stroke

### **Typography**
- SF Pro Display (titles)
- SF Pro Text (body)
- High contrast
- Follow native iOS spacing and type scale
